# Research Publications and Projects of Md. Mahfujur Rahman

## Peer-Reviewed Research Publications
Md. Mahfujur Rahman has authored and co-authored notable research papers published in prestigious IEEE international conferences and Springer Nature volumes:

1. **Polyarchitectural Deep Ensemble with Multimodal Feature Fusion for Histopathological Stratification of Colon and Lung Cancer**
   - **Authors:** Md Mahfujur Rahman, DM Asadujjaman, Md Mahmuduzzaman Ikbal, Sultan Mahmud, Mst Jannatul Ferdous, Md Toufikul Islam, Abdullah Tamim.
   - **Venue:** 2025 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT), IEEE.
   - **DOI:** 10.1109/ICCIT68739.2025.11491066
   - **Key Findings:** Developed a novel multi-architecture feature fusion framework combining EfficientNetV2 and ResNet50. The EfficientNetV2S + ResNet50 fusion model achieved a flawless 100% accuracy, while EfficientNetV2B0 + ResNet50 achieved 99.97% accuracy on lung and colon histopathology images, significantly outperforming standalone CNN baselines. This serves as the foundational research behind OncoVision AI.

2. **An Integrated Deep Learning Methodology for the Automated Histopathological Differentiation of Lung Throughout Colon Cancer**
   - **Authors:** Fahud Ahmmed, Md. Zahid Hasan, Md. Mahmuduzzaman Ikbal, Md. Mahfujur Rahman, D. M. Asadujjaman.
   - **Publisher / Venue:** Springer Nature (International Conference on Big Data, IoT and Machine Learning, 2025).
   - **DOI:** 10.1007/978-3-032-15346-3_27
   - **Key Findings:** Demonstrated an automated transfer learning approach utilizing DenseNet201, reaching 99.74% classification accuracy for differentiating complex lung and colon tissue pathologies.

3. **Transfer Learning Based Multiclass Brain Tumor Classification Using MRI Data**
   - **Authors:** Md. Mahfujur Rahman, Chinmoy Mitra, D.M. Asadujjaman, Md. Mahmuduzzaman Ikbal.
   - **Venue:** 2025 International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence, and Networking (QPAIN), IEEE.
   - **DOI:** 10.1109/QPAIN66474.2025.11172074
   - **Key Findings:** Developed an optimized ResNet50 deep learning model for automated brain tumor classification from multi-sequence MRI scans, achieving 99.31% accuracy and a 99.35% F1-score to reduce manual radiologist diagnostic turnaround times.

4. **Revolutionizing Aquatic Epidemiology: A Scalable Deep Learning Framework for Disease Detection and Enhancing Environmental Resilience**
   - **Authors:** Md Mahmuduzzaman Ikbal, Md Mahfujur Rahman, DM Asadujjaman, Abdullah Al Bassam, Jannatul Ferdous, Md Toufikul Islam, Sabina Yasmin.
   - **Venue:** 2025 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT), IEEE.

5. **Forecasting Food Prices with Transformer Models Using the Informer Approach on WFP Data**
   - **Authors:** D.M. Asadujjaman, Abdullah Tamim, Md. Mahmuduzzaman Ikbal, Md. Mahfujur Rahman, Abdullah Al Bassam.

## Major Software & AI Engineering Projects

### 1. OncoVision AI (Histopathology Cancer Classification Platform)
- **Role:** Creator, System Architect, and Lead Developer
- **Summary:** An enterprise-ready clinical decision support platform for automated lung and colon histopathology tissue classification.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - 5-Class Tissue Identification: Colon Adenocarcinoma, Colon Benign, Lung Adenocarcinoma, Lung Benign, Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma.
  - Multi-Model Ensemble Consensus: Combines MobileNetV2 (99.92%), DenseNet121 (99.44%), and EfficientNetV2B0 + ResNet50 Fusion (99.97%), yielding a combined ensemble accuracy of 99.99%.
  - Multi-Model Agreement & Confidence Scoring.
  - Generative AI & RAG: Clinical knowledge chat powered by pgvector semantic vector search and Google Gemini Flash.
  - Reporting & Analytics: Clinical PDF and CSV diagnostic report generation.
- **Tech Stack:** FastAPI, PostgreSQL, pgvector, SQLAlchemy asyncpg, TensorFlow Lite, React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Docker, Render, Netlify.
- **Repository:** [https://github.com/mahfujr403/OncoVision](https://github.com/mahfujr403/OncoVision)
- **Live Platform:** [https://oncovision-live.netlify.app/](https://oncovision-live.netlify.app/)
